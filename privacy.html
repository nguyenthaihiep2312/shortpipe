Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Information we collect

When you connect TikTok, the App receives from TikTok: your open ID, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic), and OAuth access/refresh tokens needed to upload and publish videos (scopes video.upload, video.publish).

2. How we use it

This information is used only to show which account is connected and to post the videos you choose to that account. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Storage

Tokens and account info are stored locally on the computer running the App. Videos are sent directly from your computer to TikTok.

4. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while your account is connected. Disconnecting your account in the App deletes the stored tokens. You can also revoke access in TikTok under Settings → Security → Apps and services.

5. Children

The App is not intended for users under 18.

6. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
