ShortPipe

ShortPipe is a desktop tool that turns trending topics into short videos and, with your permission, posts them to your own TikTok account using the official TikTok Content Posting API.

How it works

  1. ShortPipe renders a short video on your computer.
  2. You connect your TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit (OAuth).
  3. ShortPipe uploads the finished video to your account, either immediately or from a posting queue you control.

You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time.
